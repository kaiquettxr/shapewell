Shapewell Support

Need help with Shapewell, your subscription, or your data? Email shapewellsupport@gmail.com. Tell us what you were trying to do, what happened, and your iPhone model and iOS version if you know them. Please leave out passwords, payment details, health information, and body photos.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. Select Shapewell and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app or your Shapewell account does not cancel your subscription. Apple’s cancellation guide

How do I restore my purchase?

Use the same Apple Account you used to subscribe. In Shapewell, tap Restore on the subscription screen or open Progress → Settings → Restore purchases. If your subscription still doesn’t appear, email us.

How do I delete my account and data?

In Shapewell, open Progress, tap the Settings gear, then choose Delete account and data and follow the prompts. For other privacy or data requests, email us at the address above.
