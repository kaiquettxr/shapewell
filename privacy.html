Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Shapewell is a fitness and habits app for adults 18 and older. It is operated by Kaique Teixeira. For privacy questions or requests, email shapewellsupport@gmail.com.

Camera, scan and progress photos

Pose detection runs on your iPhone. Your original scan photos (front, side and face) and your progress photos are kept in the app on your iPhone. When you use the iOS photo picker, Shapewell receives only the photo you select. You can save progress photos to your photo library by choosing Save to Photos. We do not send your face photo or use your face to identify you.

Before your first scan, one notice explains what goes to Google Gemini. If you choose Allow there, or later turn on photo analysis in Settings, the app crops your head out of copies of the front and side photos, reduces each copy to 512 pixels on its longest side, and checks that no face is visible before sending. If the app cannot complete these checks, it does not send the photos. The copies pass through our server to Google Gemini for your report. We keep them on our server only if you separately allow team review. If you choose Not now, no photos are sent, and you can still get a report using your quiz answers and available posture measurements. Any body fat range shown is a visual estimate and is not a medical measurement.

Optional team review

After you allow photo analysis, Allow team review is a separate choice. It is off unless you opt in. If you allow it, authorized Shapewell staff can review the two cropped copies sent to Gemini and a copy of your report to check how the analysis performed. We keep this material in private server storage for up to 48 hours. Declining does not affect your report, plan, subscription or price.

You can turn off team review or photo analysis in Settings under AI & photos. This stops future team-review storage and requests deletion of the material held for team review. If deletion cannot finish immediately, the app asks you to retry. Deleting your account also deletes the material kept for team review. These controls apply to Shapewell's copies. Google handles copies it already received under its abuse-monitoring policy.

Reports, plans and the AI coach

With or without photo analysis, Google Gemini receives your quiz answers and available posture measurements to generate your report. We keep your quiz answers, including height, weight and any limitations you share, with your account. We do not store the raw posture measurements on our server. Gemini also uses relevant quiz answers and report information to write your plan.

If you choose Allow in the notice before your first scan, that also turns on Coach AI, so you may agree to share coach data with Google Gemini before you scan. If you choose Not now there, the app asks again before your first AI coach message; if you choose Not now then, the message stays unsent and you can still use the rest of the app. If we change what that notice says, the app asks you again before sending anything. You can turn off Coach AI in Settings to stop future messages from being sent. Turning off Coach AI does not delete messages already stored. For an AI reply, we send your new message, up to 10 recent chat messages, applicable quiz answers and limitations, a summary of your report, and relevant plan information. The coach does not receive photos.

If the app detects certain sensitive topics, such as pain, pregnancy or a crisis, the server may return a predefined reply. We may still save that message and include it in the recent chat sent to Gemini when you later ask for an AI reply. We keep coach messages and plan changes for up to 90 days before scheduled cleanup. You can ask us to delete your coach messages sooner at the email above. Google may keep prompts, submitted context and generated responses for up to 55 days to detect and prevent abuse. Authorized Google personnel may review flagged content under Google’s abuse-monitoring policy.

Other data, providers and retention

  • Account and app activity. Supabase hosts our account and app data. We store your quiz answers, reports, plan, and workout and habit history so you can use the app and see your progress. We generally keep those records while your account exists. If you remain anonymous and never subscribe, we remove those records after 90 days. We may retain minimal records without body data to enforce the free report limit and prevent abuse.
  • Sign-in and device verification. Sign in with Apple is optional. If you use it, we store the Apple account identifier and the relay email if Apple provides it. App Attest and DeviceCheck help verify the app and enforce the free report limit. We store an App Attest key identifier linked to your account.
  • Subscriptions. Apple processes payment. RevenueCat uses a Shapewell account identifier and subscription information to manage access and restore purchases. We do not receive card details.
  • Analytics. PostHog receives limited in-app usage events and a random installation identifier. We use these events to improve the app. We do not send your photos, quiz answers or coach messages to PostHog. We do not sell your data, use it for targeted advertising or use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Processing locations. Our service providers may process your information in the United States. We use encrypted connections and restrict access to stored data.

Your choices and rights

Use Delete account and data in Settings to delete your account and app data. If you use Sign in with Apple, we request revocation of that connection. We also request removal of your customer record from RevenueCat. Manage or cancel an active subscription in your App Store settings. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account or cancel a subscription.

You can contact us to request access, correction, a copy or deletion of your data, or to withdraw consent for a specific use where applicable. Additional rights may apply where you live. Shapewell is for adults 18 and older. If we learn we have collected data from someone under 18, we delete it. We post updates to this policy here and ask for your permission before applying changes that require it.
